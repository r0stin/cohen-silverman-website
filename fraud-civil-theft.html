Fraud & Civil Theft

When money is taken by deception.

Scams, misappropriated funds, contractors who vanish with deposits. We trace the money, freeze assets where possible and sue to get it back.

Book a consultation Call (905) 604-7662Call us

Fraud cases are a race against time. The longer money sits with the wrong person, the more likely it is to be spent, moved or hidden.

We act fast to trace where funds went and, where the evidence supports it, ask the court to freeze assets before they disappear. Then we pursue judgment and enforcement against everyone responsible.

Matters in this area are led by Benjamin Cohen and Noah Katz.

If you've just sent money to a scammer, call your bank's fraud line immediately. Fast action can sometimes stop or reverse a transfer.

What we handle

Contractors who take deposits

Builders and tradespeople who took payment and never finished, or never started.

Business partner misappropriation

Partners, directors or employees who diverted company money.

Romance and investment scams

Recovery claims where the people or receiving accounts can be identified.

Fraudulent transfers

Assets moved to relatives or new companies to avoid paying what's owed.

Asset tracing

Following the money through bank records and court-ordered disclosure.

Asset freezing orders

Urgent court orders preventing funds and property from being moved.

How we approach it

  1. Trace

    We follow the money using your records and, where needed, court-ordered disclosure from banks.

  2. Freeze

    If assets are at risk, we ask the court for an urgent order to stop them being moved.

  3. Sue

    We bring claims against everyone responsible, including those who received the money.

  4. Recover

    We enforce the judgment against accounts, property and other assets.

What to gather

  • Every payment record: transfers, receipts and card statements
  • Contracts, quotes, invoices and messages
  • Names, company names and websites used
  • Details of the accounts the money was sent to
  • Any police or bank fraud report numbers
