Serious legal help when someone has crossed a line.

Cohen & Silverman represents individuals and businesses in two kinds of matters: recovering money that is owed, and stopping people who harass, threaten, defame or deceive. These cases are personal, often urgent, and rarely straightforward.

We keep our focus narrow so we can be very good at it. Every client gets a lawyer who knows this area of law, an honest assessment before costs build up, and a clear plan for reaching an outcome.

How we help

  • Review of your contract, invoices and correspondence
  • A demand for payment, plus interest where available

  • Judgment enforcement until the debt is cleared

How we help

  • Immediate guidance: don't pay, preserve everything, lock down accounts
  • Takedown requests to platforms and hosts
  • Coordination with police
  • Urgent court orders and claims once the person is identified
Extortion & blackmail

How we help

  • A restraining or protection order, including urgent orders
  • A cease and desist letter where it's safe to send one
  • An evidence log built for court
  • Representation at the hearing, so you're never there alone
