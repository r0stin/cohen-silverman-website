A firm built for people who have been wronged.

Cohen & Silverman focuses on two things: recovering money that's owed, and protecting people from those who harass, threaten or deceive them.

{{PHOTO|shelves|eager|sizes=(max-width: 1023px) 92vw, 520px}}

We started this firm because too many people with valid claims were told their case was too small, too messy or too uncomfortable to take on.

Benjamin Cohen and Joshua Silverman built the firm around a narrow focus. Rather than doing a little of everything, we concentrate on recovery and protection matters, and we've shaped our process around how these cases actually unfold: quickly, emotionally, and often with the other side doing everything it can to avoid accountability.

That focus shapes how we work. We answer the question every client really has, whether this is worth pursuing, at the very start. We move fast when timing matters. And we stay with a matter until it's resolved, whether that means a signed settlement, a protection order or a judgment that has actually been paid.
