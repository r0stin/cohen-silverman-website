Defamation

Your reputation took years to build.

False reviews, damaging posts, lies told to clients or employers. We move to remove the content, correct the record and seek compensation.

Book a consultation Call (905) 604-7662Call us

Defamation is the publication of a false statement that harms someone's reputation. Online, it spreads quickly and stays searchable for years.

We act on two tracks: getting the content taken down or corrected as quickly as possible, and holding the person responsible. Some defamation claims have very short notice deadlines, so early advice matters.

Matters in this area are led by Joshua Silverman and Benjamin Cohen.

Some defamation claims require formal notice within weeks of the publication. Get advice early.

What we handle

Fake and malicious reviews

False reviews posted by competitors, former employees or people who were never customers.

Defamatory social media posts

Damaging false claims on social platforms, forums and comment sections.

False statements to clients or employers

Lies told to people whose opinion affects your income or career.

Impersonation

Accounts or websites pretending to be you or your business.

Takedowns and de-indexing

Requests to platforms, hosts and search engines to remove or de-list content.

Retractions and apologies

Formal demands that the person publicly correct the record.

How we approach it

  1. Preserve

    We capture the content properly, with dates and URLs, before it can be edited or deleted.

  2. Assess

    We look at whether the statement is false, harmful and actionable, and at any deadlines.

  3. Demand

    A formal letter demanding removal, retraction and an apology often resolves it.

  4. Litigate

    If needed, we sue for damages and seek court orders requiring removal.

Do this now

  • Screenshot the content with the date, time and URL visible
  • Don't respond publicly or argue in the comments
  • Note who has seen it and any effect on your work or business
  • Save evidence of lost clients, sales or opportunities
  • Contact us quickly, because notice deadlines can be short
