Debt Recovery

Recover the money you’re owed.

Unpaid loans, unpaid invoices, bounced payments, broken agreements. We demand payment, sue when needed and enforce the judgment until you're paid.

Book a consultation Call (905) 604-7662Call us

Being owed money by someone who has stopped responding is exhausting. Every week that passes makes the money harder to collect and gives the other side time to move assets or disappear.

We move quickly. We start by reviewing your paper trail and telling you honestly whether a claim makes financial sense. When it does, we send a formal demand, file in the right court, and use every available enforcement tool once you have a judgment.

Matters in this area are led by Benjamin Cohen and Noah Katz.

Limitation periods for debt claims are strict and can be as short as two years from the date payment was due. If your debt is getting old, contact us soon.

What we handle

Unpaid personal loans

Money lent to friends, family, ex-partners or acquaintances, with or without a written agreement.

Unpaid invoices

Work completed or goods delivered for clients and customers who won't pay.

Breach of contract

Deals, agreements and promises that were broken and cost you money.

Bounced and reversed payments

Bounced payments, cancelled transfers and chargebacks used to avoid paying.

Unpaid rent and damage

Amounts owed by former tenants, including arrears and repair costs.

Judgment enforcement

Garnishment, bank seizures, property liens and debtor examinations to collect what the court awarded.

How we approach it

  1. Review the evidence

    We go through your messages, transfers, contracts and invoices and confirm how much is owed and what can be proven.

  2. Demand payment

    A formal demand letter from a law firm often resolves the matter without a lawsuit.

  3. File the claim

    If they still don't pay, we sue in the appropriate court and push the case through to judgment.

  4. Enforce the judgment

    A judgment is only paper until it's collected. We pursue wages, bank accounts and property until you're paid.

What to gather before we talk

  • Texts, emails or DMs that mention the money
  • Bank statements and e-transfer records
  • Any contract, IOU, invoice or receipt
  • The other person's full name and last known address
  • A rough timeline of what was promised and when
  • Records of any partial repayments
