Extortion & Blackmail

Threatened? You don’t have to face it alone.

Sextortion, threats to expose private information, demands for money. We act immediately, contain the damage and take over communication with the person making the threats.

Book a consultation Call (905) 604-7662Call us

Extortion is designed to make you panic and pay quickly. Paying rarely ends it. More often, it leads to more demands.

Our response is fast and discreet. We help you secure your accounts and preserve evidence, coordinate with police and platforms to stop material spreading, and seek urgent court orders when needed. Where the person can be identified, we pursue them for damages.

Matters in this area are led by Joshua Silverman and Rebecca Anderson.

If the images involve someone under 18, contact police right away. If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number.

What we handle

Sextortion

Threats to share intimate images or videos unless you pay or send more.

Threats to expose private information

Demands tied to threats of revealing personal, medical, financial or family information.

Business extortion

Threats of fake reviews, data leaks or false reports unless a payment is made.

Threats from an ex-partner

Private material or information used as leverage after a relationship ends.

Data theft and ransom demands

Demands made after someone has accessed your accounts, devices or files.

Identifying the person responsible

Court orders and investigative steps to find out who is behind the threats.

How we approach it

  1. Stabilize

    We advise on immediate steps: stop engaging, lock down accounts and preserve every message.

  2. Contain

    We work with platforms and hosts to remove or block material and prevent it from spreading.

  3. Escalate

    We coordinate with police and, where needed, seek urgent court orders against publication.

  4. Hold accountable

    Once the person is identified, we can bring a civil claim for damages.

Do this now

  • Do not pay or send anything else
  • Stop replying, but do not delete the conversation
  • Screenshot the threats, usernames and profile links
  • Temporarily deactivate (don't delete) accounts the person can see
  • Report it to police and keep the report number
  • Call us before responding to any new demand
