Harassment

Make the harassment stop.

Repeated calls, messages, fake accounts, showing up uninvited. We use legal pressure, court orders and civil claims to end it and hold the person accountable.

Book a consultation Call (905) 604-7662Call us

Harassment wears people down. Whether it comes from an ex-partner, someone who lives nearby, a former business contact or an anonymous account, it affects your sleep, your work and your sense of safety.

We act on several fronts at once: a formal cease and desist letter, applications for court orders, requests to platforms to remove content and, where the law allows, a claim for damages. When the harasser is anonymous, we can ask the court to order platforms to reveal who is behind the account.

Matters in this area are led by Rebecca Anderson and Joshua Silverman.

If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number first.

What we handle

Persistent unwanted contact

Calls, texts, emails and messages that continue after you've asked them to stop.

Online harassment and cyberstalking

Abusive posts, comments, monitoring and threats across social media and messaging apps.

Fake and anonymous accounts

Impersonation and anonymous accounts used to target, embarrass or intimidate you.

Following and showing up

Waiting outside your home or workplace, or turning up wherever you'll be.

Doxxing

Publishing your address, phone number, workplace or other private information.

Harassment by an ex-partner

Ongoing contact, monitoring or intimidation after a relationship has ended.

How we approach it

  1. Document everything

    We help you build an organized record of every incident, message and account involved.

  2. Send a cease and desist

    A firm, formal letter puts the person on notice of the legal consequences of continuing.

  3. Seek court protection

    Where needed, we apply for a restraining or protection order, including on an urgent basis.

  4. Pursue accountability

    We can bring a civil claim for damages and request removal of harmful content.

Do this now

  • Don't reply or engage, even to tell them to stop again
  • Screenshot messages and posts, including dates and usernames
  • Save voicemails and call logs
  • Keep a dated log of every incident
  • Report threats of violence to police and keep the report number
  • Tell someone you trust what is happening
