Disclaimer

The content of this website is provided for general information only. It is not legal advice and should not be relied on as legal advice. You should speak with a lawyer about your specific circumstances before acting, or deciding not to act, on anything you read here.

Any descriptions of how matters are typically handled are general in nature. Every case is different, and nothing on this website is a promise or guarantee of any particular outcome.

No lawyer-client relationship

Using this website, calling our office, or sending us a message through the contact form or by email does not create a lawyer-client relationship with Cohen & Silverman Law Firm. A lawyer-client relationship is formed only once we have checked for conflicts of interest and confirmed in writing that we will act for you.

Until then, please do not send us confidential or time-sensitive documents. If you have a deadline approaching, tell us when you get in touch.

Privacy policy

We respect your privacy and handle personal information carefully, in line with our professional obligations and applicable privacy law.

What we collect

  • Information you choose to give us, such as your name, email address, phone number and a description of your matter.
  • Basic technical information that web servers record automatically, such as your browser type and the pages you visit.

How we use it

  • To respond to your message and arrange a consultation.
  • To check for conflicts of interest before we agree to act for anyone.
  • To provide legal services if you become a client, and to meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (for example, email and hosting providers), when you ask us to, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to our legal record-keeping obligations. To make a request, email cohensilvermanlaw@gmail.com.

Cookies and third parties

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. To display the site, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts, icons from the unpkg content network and photographs from Unsplash. These providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when the files load, under their own privacy policies.
